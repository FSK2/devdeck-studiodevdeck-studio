DevDeck Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Mobile One Media Services

DevDeck Studio is engineered with an uncompromising commitment to local computing, absolute keystroke privacy, and user security.

1. Zero Keystroke Logging & Zero Telemetry

DevDeck Studio operates purely as an offline-first hardware tactile deck. We do NOT track, record, capture, or store your keystrokes, clipboard text, macros, or passwords. All keystroke triggers are executed locally in real-time via Windows DirectInput APIs and Android Bluetooth HID emulation.

2. Local Direct Socket Architecture

All data exchanged between the DevDeck Android App and the DevDeck Windows PC Companion occurs strictly within your local WiFi router subnet or over a direct USB cable (ports 8989 / 8990). No connection is ever made to external cloud servers, third-party analytics, or advertising brokers.

3. Android Permissions Disclosure

• Bluetooth HID (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE): Required exclusively to identify the device as a standard tactile peripheral.
• Local Network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required strictly for local LAN / USB discovery of the companion server.
• Haptics (VIBRATE): Provides physical haptic key-click feedback.

4. Store Policy Adherence

DevDeck Studio complies with all Google Play Developer Guidelines, Google Play Data Safety policies, and Microsoft Store Quality and Privacy Standards.
