# Privacy Policy — DevDeck Studio & PC Companion

**Last Updated: September 2026**  
**Developed by: Mobile One Media Services**

Mobile One Media Services operates DevDeck Studio (Android App) and DevDeck Studio PC Companion (Windows Application). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, local networking, and privacy compliance across both the Google Play Store and Microsoft Store.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
DevDeck Studio does not collect, record, log, or sell any personal information. 
- We do **not** collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- We do **not** track location data or use third-party analytics SDKs.

## 2. Local Network (LAN) Operation Only
All communication between the DevDeck Studio Android app and the DevDeck Studio Windows PC Companion takes place exclusively over your local Wi-Fi / Local Area Network (LAN):
- Communication uses local UDP and HTTP sockets on port `8989`.
- Keystrokes, macros, and shortcut signals are transmitted directly point-to-point within your own local network.
- No keystroke data, typed code, or macro definitions are ever uploaded to cloud servers or remote databases.

## 3. DevDrop Wireless Asset Stream
The DevDrop feature allows you to send screenshots, code snippets, or files directly between your mobile device and your PC. All transfers are peer-to-peer over your local network. No intermediate cloud servers are used.

## 4. Microsoft Store & Google Play Compliance
DevDeck Studio complies fully with:
- Google Play Developer Content Policies & Data Safety section (No user data collected or shared).
- Microsoft Store Quality & Privacy Guidelines.
